# AI and advisory disclaimer

This document states how AI-generated and advisory content is used in this system and how users should treat it.

---

## Intended use

- **Responses are generated by AI** and are intended to **assist** users, not to replace authoritative sources.
- **No over-claimed data, predictions, or authoritative guarantees** are made. The system does not present outputs as official hazard declarations or regulatory determinations.
- **Users should always cross-check** advisories and heat-risk information with **PAGASA** (Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration) and other **valid, official sources** before making decisions.

---

## How the system keeps responses valid

- **Transparency:** Data sources and computation methods (e.g. heat index formula, PAGASA bands) are documented and cited where applicable.
- **No unsupported claims:** The system avoids stating outcomes or guarantees that are not backed by the documented logic or by official references.
- **Clear labeling:** Advisory and risk logic is clearly labeled (e.g. heat index vs air temperature, level names and bands) so users can understand what they are seeing.

For technical basis and citations, see **docs/HEAT-RISK-MODEL-BASIS.md** and **docs/DISCLAIMERS.md**.
